About the Event

The Accesco Living Startup Webinar is a high-octane platform designed to catalyze innovation in the Intelligent Commerce space. We bring together visionary founders, seasoned investors, and industry experts for a day of intensive pitching and networking. Our goal is to bridge the gap between groundbreaking ideas and the resources needed to scale them globally.

  • Punctuality: All teams must join the virtual room 10 minutes before their scheduled slot.

  • Media Guidelines: Ensure all slide decks are uploaded in PDF format 24 hours prior to the event.
